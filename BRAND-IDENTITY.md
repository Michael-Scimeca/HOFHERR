# Hofherr Meat Co. — Brand Identity & Lockup Standards

This document establishes the official brand identity, tagline system, and short lockups for **Hofherr Meat Co.** (Northfield & Winnetka, IL).

---

## 1. Core Brand Motto (Foundational)

> **“Quality Meats. No Compromise.”**
> 
> *The original founding principle established at 300 Happ Rd, Northfield. Used across high-level institutional branding, butcher paper roll headings, and primary storefront plaques.*

---

## 2. Top Recommendation (Flagship Narrative Lockup)

> ### **“Born in the Stockyards. Cut for the North Shore.”**
> 
> **Rationale:** Ties together Sean’s 1903 South Side wholesale butchering roots with your modern Northfield and Winnetka locations in exactly seven words.
> 
> **Primary Applications:**
> - Butcher parcel ribbon & kraft wrapping tape
> - Exterior transom glass signage & gold-leaf window decals
> - Website hero section kicker (`/`, `/our-story`, `/product-identity`)
> - Social media bio lead tagline

---

## 3. Universal Companion Lockup (Punchy Motto Pairing)

> ### **“Chicago Butchery. No Compromise.”**
> 
> **Rationale:** Direct, confident, and immediately recognizable. Acts as the punchy, authentic companion lockup alongside the founding motto.
> 
> **Primary Applications:**
> - Counter flame stickers (die-cut 3" vinyl)
> - Tamper-evident scale label headers
> - Delivery carton tape & wholesale invoice headers
> - Instagram post footer lockup

---

## 4. Short Lockups (Ideal for Stamps, Social Bios & Signage)

| # | Lockup | Recommended Medium | Brand Association |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **“Chicago Butchery · Est. 1903”** | Turned-wood rocker stamps, parcel tie tags, kraft bags | Generational family heritage; lineage back to Chicago South Side wholesale roots. |
| **02** | **“Chicago Made · Midwest Raised”** | Deli scale labels, butcher case farm placards, dry-aging tags | 100% traceable regional family farms (IL, WI, IA) + in-house Chicago preparation. |
| **03** | **“Craft Butchery. Chicago Heritage.”** | Social bios (@hofherrmeatco), email signatures, web headers | Elevated culinary butchery meets 120+ years of regional butchering authority. |
| **04** | **“A True Chicago Original.”** | Gold-leaf storefront window graphics, sidewalk A-frames | Distinctive North Shore destination; counter experience impossible in supermarkets. |

## 6. Typography & Color Specifications

- **Headline Lockups:** `Yanone Kaffeesatz` (`font-weight: 700 / 800`, all uppercase, `letter-spacing: 0.02em–0.05em`)
- **Body & Editorial Specs:** `Inter` (`font-weight: 400 / 500 / 600`)
- **Primary Ink:** Oxblood Crimson (`#8B0A10` / `var(--red-dark)`)
- **Secondary Accent:** Smokehouse Amber (`#F59B1A` / `var(--gold)`)
- **Depot Accent:** Depot Heritage Green (`#142A1E`)
- **Dark Ground:** Smokehouse Charcoal (`#1C1614` / `#000000`)
- **Light Ground:** Butcher Counter Cream (`#F3EAD8`) or Classic Kraft (`#C8A47E`)
